About Me

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Arkansas State University and an undergraduate research assistant advised by Prof. Donghoon Kim .

My research interests include Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and Video Understanding. I am particularly interested in using visual information to understand human and object behaviors, interactions, and their evolution over time.

My long-term goal is to connect visual understanding with decision-making and action in embodied AI systems.
